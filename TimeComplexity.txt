The loop stops when:    j>n
We know:   j=2^k
where k is the number of iterations.     So:  2^k > n

Now we need to find k.

Take log base 2 on both sides:
log2(2^k) > log2(n)
The logarithm cancels the exponent:     k > log2(n) 
Therefore:   k=log2(n)
